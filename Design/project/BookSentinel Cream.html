/08 · Intel · FAQ

Common transmissions.

How fast is a takedown actually executed?

Median MTTR is 42 minutes from operator swipe to host acknowledgment. Hosts with DMCA SLAs respond within 14 minutes on average.

What jurisdictions do you operate in?

We route through DMCA (US), Article 17 (EU), Section 79 (IN), and 14 other regional frameworks. Sovereign tier supports custom counsel.

Is the registry court-admissible?

Yes. Every event is signed, timestamped by RFC 3161 TSAs, and exportable as an evidentiary bundle.

Who is BookSentinel built for?

Traditional book publishers, literary imprints, and rights agencies. We focus on print and ebook piracy of trade titles — not audiobooks, screenplays, music, or video.

What does Gemini do in the loop?

Triage. Gemini classifies candidates, fingerprints content, and pre-scores risk — so your operators only see signal.

Can I trial without a credit card?

Yes. 14-day clearance on the Rights Manager tier. We'll provision a sandbox sector with synthetic threats.
